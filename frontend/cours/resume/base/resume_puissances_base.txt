===Résumé Puissances

Titre: Résumé : Puissances

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définitions fondamentales</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Notation</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^n$ ($\displaystyle n \geq 1$)</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a \times a \times a \times \cdots \times a$ ($\displaystyle n$ fois)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^0$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle 1$ (si $\displaystyle a \neq 0$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^{-n}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \frac{1}{a^n}$ (si $\displaystyle a \neq 0$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^1$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-radius:6px; border-left:6px solid #ffa500; padding:15px; margin:12px 0;">
<strong>⚠️ Attention :</strong> $\displaystyle 0^0$ n'est <strong>pas défini</strong> ! Tout nombre non nul élevé à la puissance 0 vaut 1.
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Propriétés des puissances</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:40%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Produit de puissances (même base)</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Quotient de puissances (même base)</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Puissance de puissance</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (a^m)^n = a^{m \times n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Puissance d'un produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (a \times b)^n = a^n \times b^n$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Puissance d'un quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^n = \frac{a^n}{b^n}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#f0f9f0; border-radius:6px; border-left:6px solid #10b981; padding:15px; margin:12px 0;">
<strong>Méthode :</strong> Pour <strong>multiplier</strong> : on additionne les exposants • Pour <strong>diviser</strong> : on soustrait les exposants • Pour une <strong>puissance de puissance</strong> : on multiplie les exposants
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Notation scientifique</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Élément</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Forme générale</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a \times 10^n$ avec $\displaystyle 1 \leq a < 10$ et $\displaystyle n \in \mathbb{Z}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Grand nombre</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Exposant positif (ex: $\displaystyle 3\,500 = 3{,}5 \times 10^3$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Petit nombre</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Exposant négatif (ex: $\displaystyle 0{,}0042 = 4{,}2 \times 10^{-3}$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (a \times 10^m) \times (b \times 10^n) = (a \times b) \times 10^{m+n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \frac{a \times 10^m}{b \times 10^n} = \frac{a}{b} \times 10^{m-n}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:25%;">Base</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:25%;">Exposant</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Résultat</th>

<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">

<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-radius:6px; border-left:6px solid #ffa500; padding:15px; margin:12px 0;">

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Erreurs fréquentes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ef4444; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:50%;">❌ Erreur</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">✓ Correct</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^m \times a^n = a^{mn}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (a^m)^n = a^{m+n}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (a^m)^n = a^{m \times n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (a + b)^n = a^n + b^n$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Il faut développer ! (sauf cas particuliers)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^{-n} = -a^n$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle 2^3 = 6$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle 2^3 = 8$ (c'est $\displaystyle 2 \times 3 = 6$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle -2^2 = 4$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle -2^2 = -4$ (c'est $\displaystyle (-2)^2 = 4$)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Méthode de calcul</h2>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#f59e0b; color:white;">

<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>1</strong></td>

<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>3</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Simplifier les exposants (additionner, soustraire ou multiplier selon le cas)</td>

<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>4</strong></td>

<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>5</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Vérifier les signes (attention aux bases négatives)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<h3 style="color:#2c3e50; margin-top:0;">✅ Points clés à retenir</h3>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Notion</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">À retenir</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Définition</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^n$ = produit de $\displaystyle n$ facteurs égaux à $\displaystyle a$ ; $\displaystyle a^0 = 1$ ; $\displaystyle a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a^m \times a^n = a^{m+n}$ (on additionne les exposants)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n}$ (on soustrait les exposants)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Puissance de puissance</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (a^m)^n = a^{m \times n}$ (on multiplie les exposants)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Distribution</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (ab)^n = a^n b^n$ et $\displaystyle \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^n = \frac{a^n}{b^n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Notation scientifique</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a \times 10^n$ avec $\displaystyle 1 \leq a < 10$ et $\displaystyle n \in \mathbb{Z}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Signes</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Base négative avec exposant pair → positif ; exposant impair → négatif</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Attention parenthèses</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle -a^n \neq (-a)^n$ ! Les parenthèses sont essentielles</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
