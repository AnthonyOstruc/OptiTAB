===Résumé Structures Conditionnelles en Python

Titre: Résumé : Structures Conditionnelles en Python

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Opérateurs de comparaison</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Opérateur</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Signification</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Exemple</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>==</code></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Égal à</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>5 == 5</code> → <code>True</code></td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>!=</code></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Différent de</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>5 != 3</code> → <code>True</code></td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>&lt;</code></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Strictement inférieur</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>3 &lt; 7</code> → <code>True</code></td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>&gt;</code></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Strictement supérieur</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>8 &gt; 10</code> → <code>False</code></td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>&lt;=</code></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Inférieur ou égal</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>5 &lt;= 5</code> → <code>True</code></td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>&gt;=</code></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Supérieur ou égal</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><code>4 &gt;= 9</code> → <code>False</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#fef3c7; border-radius:6px; border-left:6px solid #ffa500; padding:15px; margin:12px 0;">
<strong>⚠️ À ne pas confondre :</strong><br><br>
• <code>=</code> : affectation (<code>x = 5</code>)<br><br>
• <code>==</code> : comparaison (<code>x == 5</code>)
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Structures conditionnelles</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<h3 style="color:#34495e; margin-top:0;">1. Structure <code>if</code></h3>
<div style="background:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0; font-family:monospace;">
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> condition:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instruction1<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instruction2
</div>
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; padding:10px; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0;">
<strong>Usage :</strong> Exécute le bloc d'instructions uniquement si la condition est vraie
</div>

<h3 style="color:#34495e; margin-top:20px;">2. Structure <code>if... else</code></h3>
<div style="background:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0; font-family:monospace;">
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> condition:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instructions <span style="color:#6272a4;"># si vraie</span><br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">else</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instructions <span style="color:#6272a4;"># si fausse</span>
</div>
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; padding:10px; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0;">
<strong>Usage :</strong> Deux alternatives possibles - un seul des deux blocs sera exécuté
</div>

<h3 style="color:#34495e; margin-top:20px;">3. Structure <code>if... elif... else</code></h3>
<div style="background:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0; font-family:monospace;">
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> condition1:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instructions<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">elif</span> condition2:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instructions<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">elif</span> condition3:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instructions<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">else</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;instructions <span style="color:#6272a4;"># par défaut</span>
</div>
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; padding:10px; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0;">
<strong>Usage :</strong> Plusieurs conditions testées dans l'ordre. Dès qu'une condition est vraie, son bloc est exécuté et les autres sont ignorés
</div>

<div style="background:#fef3c7; padding:12px; border-radius:4px; margin:15px 0; border-left:6px solid #f59e0b;">
<strong>⚠️ Points essentiels en Python :</strong><br>
• <strong>Deux-points <code>:</code></strong> obligatoires après <code>if</code>, <code>elif</code>, <code>else</code><br>
• <strong>Indentation</strong> (4 espaces) obligatoire pour définir les blocs<br>
• Les conditions sont testées <strong>dans l'ordre</strong>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Exemples types</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<h3 style="color:#34495e; margin-top:0;">Test de majorité</h3>
<div style="background:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0; font-family:monospace;">
age = <span style="color:#bd93f9;">17</span><br>
<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> age >= <span style="color:#bd93f9;">18</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#50fa7b;">print</span>(<span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Majeur"</span>)<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">else</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#50fa7b;">print</span>(<span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Mineur"</span>)
</div>
<div style="background:#e8f5e8; padding:8px; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0;">
<strong>Résultat :</strong> "Mineur" (car 17 &lt; 18)
</div>

<h3 style="color:#34495e; margin-top:20px;">Attribution de mention</h3>
<div style="background:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0; font-family:monospace;">
moyenne = <span style="color:#bd93f9;">14.5</span><br>
<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> moyenne >= <span style="color:#bd93f9;">16</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;mention = <span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Très Bien"</span><br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">elif</span> moyenne >= <span style="color:#bd93f9;">14</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;mention = <span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Bien"</span><br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">elif</span> moyenne >= <span style="color:#bd93f9;">12</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;mention = <span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Assez Bien"</span><br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">elif</span> moyenne >= <span style="color:#bd93f9;">10</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;mention = <span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Passable"</span><br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">else</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;mention = <span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Échec"</span>
</div>
<div style="background:#e8f5e8; padding:8px; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0;">
<strong>Résultat :</strong> "Bien" (14.5 >= 14)
</div>

<h3 style="color:#34495e; margin-top:20px;">Conditions combinées</h3>
<div style="background:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0; font-family:monospace;">
age = <span style="color:#bd93f9;">23</span><br>
inscrit = <span style="color:#bd93f9;">True</span><br>
<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> age < <span style="color:#bd93f9;">26</span> <span style="color:#ff79c6;">and</span> inscrit == <span style="color:#bd93f9;">True</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#50fa7b;">print</span>(<span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Réduction accordée"</span>)<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">else</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#50fa7b;">print</span>(<span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Pas de réduction"</span>)
</div>
<div style="background:#e8f5e8; padding:8px; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0;">
<strong>Analyse :</strong> (23 &lt; 26) <code>and</code> (True) = True <code>and</code> True = <strong>True</strong><br>
<strong>Résultat :</strong> "Réduction accordée"
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Conditions imbriquées</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<p>On peut placer des structures conditionnelles à l'intérieur d'autres structures conditionnelles.</p>

<div style="background:#2c3e50; color:#ffffff; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0; font-family:monospace;">
age = <span style="color:#bd93f9;">10</span><br>
taille = <span style="color:#bd93f9;">145</span><br>
<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> age >= <span style="color:#bd93f9;">12</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#50fa7b;">print</span>(<span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Toutes les attractions"</span>)<br>
<span style="color:#ff79c6;">else</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#ff79c6;">if</span> taille >= <span style="color:#bd93f9;">140</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#50fa7b;">print</span>(<span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Attractions moyennes"</span>)<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#ff79c6;">else</span>:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<span style="color:#50fa7b;">print</span>(<span style="color:#f1fa8c;">"Attractions enfants"</span>)
</div>

<div style="background:#fef3c7; padding:10px; border-radius:4px; margin:10px 0; border-left:6px solid #f59e0b;">
<strong>⚠️ Attention à l'indentation :</strong><br>
• Premier niveau : 4 espaces<br>
• Deuxième niveau (imbriqué) : 8 espaces<br>
• L'indentation doit être cohérente !
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Méthodologie générale</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fef3c7; padding:15px; border-radius:6px; margin:10px 0;">
<strong>📐 Démarche pour créer une structure conditionnelle :</strong>
<ol style="margin:10px 0; padding-left:20px;">
    <li><strong>Identifier</strong> les conditions à tester</li>
    <li><strong>Choisir</strong> la structure appropriée (<code>if</code>, <code>if...else</code>, <code>if...elif...else</code>)</li>
    <li><strong>Ordonner</strong> les conditions du plus spécifique au plus général</li>
    <li><strong>Ajouter</strong> les deux-points après chaque condition</li>
    <li><strong>Indenter</strong> correctement (4 espaces par niveau)</li>
    <li><strong>Tester</strong> avec plusieurs valeurs différentes</li>
</ol>
</div>
</div>
