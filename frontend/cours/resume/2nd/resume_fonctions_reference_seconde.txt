<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Fonction affine (rappel)</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Notion</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">À retenir</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Définition</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle f(x)=ax+b$ sur $\displaystyle \mathbb{R}$, avec $\displaystyle a$ coefficient directeur, $\displaystyle b$ ordonnée à l'origine.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Variations</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle a>0$ : croissante ; $\displaystyle a<0$ : décroissante ; $\displaystyle a=0$ : constante.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Courbe</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">Droite passant par $\displaystyle (0\,;\,b)$.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Résoudre $\displaystyle f(x)=k$ et $\displaystyle f(x)&lt;k$</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(245,158,11,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #f59e0b 0%, #d97706 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Équation / inéquation</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Solutions</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fff9e6;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle x^2=k$</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle k>0$ : $\displaystyle x=\sqrt{k}$ ou $\displaystyle x=-\sqrt{k}$ ; $\displaystyle k=0$ : $\displaystyle x=0$ ; $\displaystyle k<0$ : ∅.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle x^2&lt;k$</strong> $(k>0)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle -\sqrt{k}&lt;x&lt;\sqrt{k}$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fff9e6;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle \frac{1}{x}=k$</strong> $(k\ne 0)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle x=\frac{1}{k}$ (et $\displaystyle x\ne 0$).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle \sqrt{x}=k$</strong> $(k\geq 0)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle x=k^2$ (et $\displaystyle x\geq 0$).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fff9e6;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle \sqrt{x}&lt;k$</strong> $(k>0)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle 0\leq x&lt;k^2$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle x^3=k$</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle x=\sqrt[3]{k}$ (solution unique).</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0 0 0; border:2px solid #10b981; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(16,185,129,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle S=\{-3\,;\,3\}$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle x^2<9$</td>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Comparer $\displaystyle f(a)$ et $\displaystyle f(b)$</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Méthode</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Démarche</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Par les variations</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">Si $\displaystyle f$ croissante et $\displaystyle a&lt;b$ → $\displaystyle f(a)&lt;f(b)$.<br>Si $\displaystyle f$ décroissante et $\displaystyle a&lt;b$ → $\displaystyle f(a)>f(b)$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Graphiquement</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">Placer $\displaystyle A(a\,;\,f(a))$ et $\displaystyle B(b\,;\,f(b))$ sur la courbe, comparer les ordonnées.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>⚠️ Fonction carré</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">S'assurer que $\displaystyle a$ et $\displaystyle b$ sont <strong>du même côté</strong> de 0 (sinon calculer directement).</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VIII. Position relative des courbes $\displaystyle y=x$, $\displaystyle y=x^2$, $\displaystyle y=x^3$</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Intervalle ($\displaystyle x\geq 0$)</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Ordre</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle 0\leq x<1$</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle x^3\leq x^2\leq x$ (la droite $\displaystyle y=x$ est au-dessus).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle x=1$</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">Les trois courbes se croisent au point $\displaystyle (1\,;\,1)$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle x>1$</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle x\leq x^2\leq x^3$ (la cubique $\displaystyle y=x^3$ est au-dessus).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Démonstration</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle x^2-x=x(x-1)$ et $\displaystyle x^3-x^2=x^2(x-1)$ : signe dépend de $\displaystyle (x-1)$.</td>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">X. Erreurs à éviter</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">❌ Erreur</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">✅ Bon réflexe</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier $\displaystyle x\ne 0$ pour $\displaystyle \frac{1}{x}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Toujours vérifier le <strong>domaine de définition</strong>.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier $\displaystyle x\geq 0$ pour $\displaystyle \sqrt{x}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{x}$ n'existe que pour $\displaystyle x\geq 0$ dans $\displaystyle \mathbb{R}$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Écrire $\displaystyle x^2=k \Rightarrow x=\sqrt{k}$ (une seule solution)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle x^2=k$ a <strong>deux solutions</strong> si $\displaystyle k>0$ : $\displaystyle \pm\sqrt{k}$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Confondre paire et impaire</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Paire</strong> : symétrie / axe $\displaystyle Oy$. <strong>Impaire</strong> : symétrie / origine $\displaystyle O$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Dire que $\displaystyle \frac{1}{x}$ est «décroissante sur $\displaystyle \mathbb{R}^*$»</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Décroissante sur <strong>chaque intervalle</strong> séparément ($\displaystyle ]-\infty;0[$ et $\displaystyle ]0;+\infty[$).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier que l'ordre change autour de $\displaystyle x=1$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle x^3&lt;x^2&lt;x$ si $\displaystyle 0&lt;x&lt;1$, mais $\displaystyle x&lt;x^2&lt;x^3$ si $\displaystyle x>1$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Pour $\displaystyle \frac{1}{x}>k$, ne pas séparer les cas $\displaystyle x>0$ / $\displaystyle x<0$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Étudier <strong>chaque cas séparément</strong> (le sens de l'inégalité dépend du signe de $\displaystyle x$).</td>
</tr>
