===Résumé Suites Numériques


Titre: Résumé : Suites Numériques 

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définitions essentielles</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:25%;">Type de suite</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Suite explicite</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_n = f(n)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Le terme dépend directement de $\displaystyle n$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Suite récurrente</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \begin{cases} u_0 = a \\ u_{n+1} = f(u_n) \end{cases}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Chaque terme dépend du précédent</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Notation des sommes</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle S_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} u_k$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Somme des <strong>n+1</strong> premiers termes, de $\displaystyle u_0$ à $\displaystyle u_n$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Suites arithmétiques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Définition</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_{n+1} = u_n + r$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Raison $\displaystyle r$ constante</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Terme général</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_n = u_0 + n\,r$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Expression explicite du terme de rang $\displaystyle n$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Relation générale</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_k = u_m + (k-m)\,r$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Passage d'un terme à un autre</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Somme partielle</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle S_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} u_k = \frac{(n+1)(u_0 + u_n)}{2}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Somme des $\displaystyle n+1$ premiers termes</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Suites géométriques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Définition</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_{n+1} = q\,u_n$ avec $\displaystyle q\neq 0$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Raison $\displaystyle q$ constante</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Terme général</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_n = u_0\,q^n$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Expression explicite du terme de rang $\displaystyle n$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Relation générale</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_k = u_m\,q^{\,k-m}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Passage d'un terme à un autre</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ffa500; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Cas de la somme</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Si $\displaystyle q = 1$</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle S_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} u_k = (n+1)\,u_0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Si $\displaystyle q \neq 1$</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle S_n = \sum_{k=0}^{n} u_k = u_0\,\frac{1 - q^{\,n+1}}{1 - q} = u_0\,\frac{q^{\,n+1} - 1}{q - 1}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Suite arithmético-géométrique</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Forme</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_{n+1} = a\,u_n + b$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Avec $\displaystyle a\neq 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Point fixe</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \ell = \dfrac{b}{1-a}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Si $\displaystyle a\neq 1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Réduction</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Poser $\displaystyle v_n = u_n - \ell$ ⟹ $\displaystyle v_{n+1}=a\,v_n$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Suite géométrique de raison $\displaystyle a$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Terme général</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \boxed{\,u_n = \ell + (u_0 - \ell)\,a^n\,}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Formule finale après réduction</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Condition sur $\displaystyle a$</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Convergence</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle |a|<1$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_n \to \ell$ (converge vers le point fixe)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle |a|>1$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle |u_n|$ diverge en général (sauf cas particuliers)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle a=1$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u_{n+1}=u_n+b$ (suite arithmétique)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Tableau récapitulatif — sens de variation</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Type de suite</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Condition</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Variation</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Arithmétique</strong><br>$\displaystyle u_{n+1} = u_n + r$</td>

$\displaystyle r > 0$<br>
$\displaystyle r = 0$<br>
$\displaystyle r < 0$
</td>

↗ Croissante<br>

</td>
</tr>

$\displaystyle q > 1$<br>
$\displaystyle q = 1$<br>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Raisonnement par récurrence</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#f59e0b; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:20%;">Étape</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Action</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>1. Initialisation</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Vérifier $\displaystyle P(n_0)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Montrer que la propriété est vraie pour $\displaystyle n_0$ (souvent $\displaystyle 0$ ou $\displaystyle 1$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>2. Hérédité</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle P(k) \Rightarrow P(k+1)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Supposer $\displaystyle P(k)$ vraie pour un $\displaystyle k\ge n_0$, montrer $\displaystyle P(k+1)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>3. Conclusion</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Par récurrence</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle P(n)$ est vraie pour tout $\displaystyle n\ge n_0$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VIII. Formules rapides — mémo</h2>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:8px;"><strong>Somme arithmétique</strong></td>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:8px;"><strong>Terme général (géom.)</strong></td>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:8px;"><strong>Somme des $\displaystyle n$ premiers entiers</strong></td>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:8px;"><strong>Point fixe (arithm.-géom.)</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:8px;">$\displaystyle \ell = \dfrac{b}{1-a}$ pour $\displaystyle u_{n+1}=a u_n+b$ (si $a\neq 1$)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
