    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Règles de négation</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
        <table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
            <thead>
                <tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:40%;">Proposition</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Négation</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \forall x, P(x)$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \exists x, \neg P(x)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \exists x, P(x)$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \forall x, \neg P(x)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \exists! x, P(x)$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (\forall x, \neg P(x)) \text{ ou } (\exists x, y, x \neq y \text{ et } P(x) \text{ et } P(y))$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle P \Rightarrow Q$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle P \text{ et } \neg Q$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle P \land Q$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \neg P \lor \neg Q$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle P \lor Q$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \neg P \land \neg Q$</td>
                </tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Modes de raisonnement</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
        <table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b;">
            <thead>
                <tr style="background:#f59e0b; color:white;">
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Méthode</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Principe</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Disjonction des cas</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">On considère tous les cas possibles qui recouvrent la situation</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Contraposition</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Pour prouver $\displaystyle P \Rightarrow Q$, on prouve $\displaystyle \neg Q \Rightarrow \neg P$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Par l'absurde</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Pour prouver $\displaystyle P$, on suppose $\displaystyle \neg P$ et on aboutit à une contradiction</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Analyse-synthèse</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Analyse : conditions nécessaires. Synthèse : vérification des solutions</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Récurrence simple</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Initialisation : $\displaystyle P(0)$. Hérédité : $\displaystyle P(n) \Rightarrow P(n+1)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Récurrence forte</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Initialisation : $\displaystyle P(0)$. Hérédité : $\displaystyle (\forall k \leq n, P(k)) \Rightarrow P(n+1)$</td>
                </tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>
    </div>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Opérations sur les ensembles</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
        <table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
            <thead>
                <tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:25%;">Opération</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Notation</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Réunion</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle A \cup B$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \{x \in E \mid x \in A \text{ ou } x \in B\}$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Intersection</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle A \cap B$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \{x \in E \mid x \in A \text{ et } x \in B\}$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Différence</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle A \setminus B$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \{x \in E \mid x \in A \text{ et } x \notin B\}$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Complémentaire</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \overline{A}$, $\displaystyle A^c$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \{x \in E \mid x \notin A\}$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Différence symétrique</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle A \triangle B$</td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (A \setminus B) \cup (B \setminus A)$</td>
                </tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>
    </div>

    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
        <table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9c27b0;">
            <thead>
                <tr style="background:#9c27b0; color:white;">
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Propriété</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Commutativité</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle A \cup B = B \cup A$, $\displaystyle A \cap B = B \cap A$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Associativité</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (A \cup B) \cup C = A \cup (B \cup C)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Distributivité</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle A \cap (B \cup C) = (A \cap B) \cup (A \cap C)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Lois de De Morgan</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \overline{A \cup B} = \overline{A} \cap \overline{B}$, $\displaystyle \overline{A \cap B} = \overline{A} \cup \overline{B}$</td>
                </tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>
    </div>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Applications</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
        <table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
            <thead>
                <tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Propriété</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Injection</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \forall x, y \in E, \quad f(x) = f(y) \Rightarrow x = y$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Surjection</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \forall y \in F, \quad \exists x \in E, \quad f(x) = y$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Bijection</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Injective et surjective</td>
                </tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>
    </div>

    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

            <thead>
                <tr style="background:#9c27b0; color:white;">
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:40%;">Opération</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Propriété</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Image directe</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f(A \cup B) = f(A) \cup f(B)$, $\displaystyle f(A \cap B) \subset f(A) \cap f(B)$ (égalité si injective)</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Image réciproque</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f^{-1}(B \cup C) = f^{-1}(B) \cup f^{-1}(C)$, $\displaystyle f^{-1}(B \cap C) = f^{-1}(B) \cap f^{-1}(C)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Composition</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Composée de deux injections est injective, composée de deux surjections est surjective</td>
                </tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Relations</h2>

                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Propriété</th>

                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \forall x \in E, \quad x \mathcal{R} x$</td>

                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">

                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Transitive</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \forall x, y, z \in E, \quad (x \mathcal{R} y \text{ et } y \mathcal{R} z) \Rightarrow x \mathcal{R} z$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Antisymétrique</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \forall x, y \in E, \quad (x \mathcal{R} y \text{ et } y \mathcal{R} x) \Rightarrow x = y$</td>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Points clés à retenir</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
        <table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; border:2px solid #3498db;">
            <thead>
                <tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Notion</th>
                    <th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">À retenir</th>
                </tr>
            </thead>
            <tbody>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Quantificateurs</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">L'ordre est crucial : $\displaystyle \forall x, \exists y$ n'est pas équivalent à $\displaystyle \exists y, \forall x$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Négation</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \neg(\forall x, P(x)) \Leftrightarrow \exists x, \neg P(x)$ et $\displaystyle \neg(\exists x, P(x)) \Leftrightarrow \forall x, \neg P(x)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Contraposition</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (P \Rightarrow Q) \Leftrightarrow (\neg Q \Rightarrow \neg P)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Récurrence</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Ne jamais oublier l'initialisation. Hérédité : $\displaystyle P(n) \Rightarrow P(n+1)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Opérations sur les ensembles</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Lois de De Morgan : $\displaystyle \overline{A \cup B} = \overline{A} \cap \overline{B}$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Image directe/réciproque</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f(A \cap B) \subset f(A) \cap f(B)$ (égalité si injective). $\displaystyle f^{-1}(B \cap C) = f^{-1}(B) \cap f^{-1}(C)$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Bijection</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Une bijection admet une réciproque unique. $\displaystyle (g \circ f)^{-1} = f^{-1} \circ g^{-1}$</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Classes d'équivalence</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Les classes d'équivalence forment une partition de l'ensemble sous-jacent</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Partition</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Recouvrement disjoint où aucune partie n'est vide</td>
                </tr>
                <tr style="background:#ffffff;">
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Erreurs fréquentes</strong></td>
                    <td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Confondre $\displaystyle \in$ et $\displaystyle \subset$, utiliser $\displaystyle f^{-1}$ sans vérifier la bijectivité, oublier l'initialisation en récurrence</td>
                </tr>
            </tbody>
        </table>
    </div>
