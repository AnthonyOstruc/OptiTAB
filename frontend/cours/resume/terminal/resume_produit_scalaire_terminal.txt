Titre: Résumé : Produit Scalaire et Orthogonalité

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définition du produit scalaire</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Type</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Définition géométrique</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \|\vec{u}\| \times \|\vec{v}\| \times \cos(\theta)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Définition analytique</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = x_1x_2 + y_1y_2$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Cas particuliers</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
<strong>Orthogonaux :</strong> $\displaystyle\vec{u} \perp \vec{v} \Leftrightarrow \vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$<br><br>
<strong>Même sens :</strong> $\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \|\vec{u}\| \times \|\vec{v}\|$<br><br>
<strong>Sens opposés :</strong> $\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = -\|\vec{u}\| \times \|\vec{v}\|$
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Propriétés du produit scalaire</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Expression</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Commutativité</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = \vec{v} \cdot \vec{u}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Bilinéarité</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle(k\vec{u}) \cdot \vec{v} = k(\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v})$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Distributivité</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot (\vec{v} + \vec{w}) = \vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} + \vec{u} \cdot \vec{w}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Carré scalaire</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{u} = \|\vec{u}\|^2$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#e8f5e8; border-radius:6px; border-left:6px solid #3498db; padding:15px; margin:12px 0;">
<strong>Conséquence importante :</strong> $\displaystyle\|\vec{u}\| = \sqrt{\vec{u} \cdot \vec{u}}$
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Orthogonalité</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Deux vecteurs $\displaystyle\vec{u}$ et $\displaystyle\vec{v}$ sont orthogonaux si et seulement si $\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 0$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#f59e0b; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:15%;">Étape</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Méthode de vérification</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>1</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Calculer $\displaystyle\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>2</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Si résultat = 0 → orthogonaux</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>3</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Si résultat ≠ 0 → non orthogonaux</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-radius:6px; border-left:6px solid #ffa500; padding:15px; margin:12px 0;">
<strong>⚠️ Attention :</strong> Le vecteur nul est orthogonal à tout vecteur. Pour des vecteurs non nuls, "orthogonal" = "perpendiculaire".
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Norme d'un vecteur</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Pour $\displaystyle\vec{u} = \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}$ : $\displaystyle\|\vec{u}\| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Propriété</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\|\vec{u}\|^2 = \vec{u} \cdot \vec{u} = x^2 + y^2$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ffa500; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Exemple</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\vec{u} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$ → $\displaystyle\|\vec{u}\| = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{25} = 5$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Projection orthogonale</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Projection orthogonale de $\displaystyle\vec{v}$ sur $\displaystyle\vec{u}$ : $\displaystyle\text{proj}_{\vec{u}}(\vec{v}) = \frac{\vec{v} \cdot \vec{u}}{\|\vec{u}\|^2} \vec{u}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Application</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Donne la composante de $\displaystyle\vec{v}$ dans la direction de $\displaystyle\vec{u}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ffa500; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Exemple</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Si $\displaystyle\vec{u} = \begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ 4 \end{pmatrix}$ et $\displaystyle\vec{v} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}$, alors $\displaystyle\text{proj}_{\vec{u}}(\vec{v}) = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{6}{5} \\ \frac{8}{5} \end{pmatrix}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Applications géométriques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Application</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Distance entre points</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle AB = \sqrt{(x_B - x_A)^2 + (y_B - y_A)^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Angle entre vecteurs</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\cos(\theta) = \frac{\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}}{\|\vec{u}\| \times \|\vec{v}\|}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Vecteur unitaire</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\vec{u}_0 = \frac{\vec{u}}{\|\vec{u}\|}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Classification des angles</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
$\displaystyle\cos(\theta) > 0$ → angle aigu ($\displaystyle\theta < 90°$)<br><br>
$\displaystyle\cos(\theta) = 0$ → angle droit ($\displaystyle\theta = 90°$)<br><br>
$\displaystyle\cos(\theta) < 0$ → angle obtus ($\displaystyle\theta > 90°$)
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Équations de droites et distances</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Type</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Équation cartésienne</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle ax + by + c = 0$ (avec $\displaystyle\vec{n} = \begin{pmatrix} a \\ b \end{pmatrix}$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Distance point-droite</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle d(A, \text{droite}) = \frac{|ax_0 + by_0 + c|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Distance point-plan (3D)</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle d(A, \text{plan}) = \frac{|ax_0 + by_0 + cz_0 + d|}{\sqrt{a^2 + b^2 + c^2}}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-radius:6px; border-left:6px solid #ffa500; padding:15px; margin:12px 0;">
<strong>⚠️ Important :</strong> Le vecteur normal $\displaystyle\vec{n}$ est orthogonal à la droite. Ne pas confondre avec le vecteur directeur !
</div>
</div>
