===Résumé Calcul Littéral

Titre: Résumé : Calcul littéral — puissances, racines, identités remarquables, fractions, équations/inéquations (Seconde)

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin-bottom:20px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.05em; color:#2c3e50;"><strong>📚 Objectif :</strong> Simplifier et transformer des expressions (développer / réduire / factoriser), appliquer les règles sur <strong>puissances</strong> et <strong>racines</strong>, gérer les <strong>fractions littérales</strong> (valeurs interdites), résoudre des <strong>équations/inéquations du 1er degré</strong> et isoler une variable.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Puissances</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Règle</th>

<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Exposant zéro / négatif</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle a^0=1$ et $\displaystyle a^{-n}=\frac{1}{a^n}$ (si $\displaystyle a\ne 0$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Produit</strong> (même base)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle a^m\times a^n=a^{m+n}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Quotient</strong> (même base)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \frac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n}$ (si $\displaystyle a\ne 0$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Puissance de puissance</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle (a^m)^n=a^{mn}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Puissance d’un produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle (ab)^n=a^n b^n$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Puissance d’un quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \left(\frac{a}{b}\right)^n=\frac{a^n}{b^n}$ (si $\displaystyle b\ne 0$)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Racines carrées</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(16,185,129,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #10b981 0%, #059669 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule (et conditions)</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#ecfdf5;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Définition</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{a}$ existe si $\displaystyle a\ge 0$ ; et $\displaystyle (\sqrt{a})^2=a$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{ab}=\sqrt{a}\sqrt{b}$ (si $\displaystyle a\ge 0$ et $\displaystyle b\ge 0$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ecfdf5;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{\frac{a}{b}}=\frac{\sqrt{a}}{\sqrt{b}}$ (si $\displaystyle a\ge 0$ et $\displaystyle b&gt;0$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Valeur absolue</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{a^2}=|a|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ecfdf5;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Extraction</strong> (carré parfait)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{k^2a}=k\sqrt{a}$ (si $\displaystyle k\ge 0$ et $\displaystyle a\ge 0$) • ex : $\displaystyle \sqrt{72}=6\sqrt{2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Rationaliser</strong> (cas simple)</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \frac{3}{\sqrt{5}}=\frac{3\sqrt{5}}{5}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VIII. Erreurs à éviter</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">❌ Erreur</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">✅ Correction / réflexe</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle 0^0=1$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle 0^0$ n’est <strong>pas défini</strong>.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle -2^4=16$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle -2^4=-(2^4)$ ; pour $\displaystyle 16$ : $\displaystyle (-2)^4$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle (a+b)^2=a^2+b^2$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle (a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2$ (il manque $\displaystyle 2ab$).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{a+b}=\sqrt{a}+\sqrt{b}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Faux (en général) : on ne “sépare” pas une racine d’une somme.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{a^2}=a$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{a^2}=|a|$.</td>
</tr>
