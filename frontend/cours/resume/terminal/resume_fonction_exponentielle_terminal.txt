===Résumé Fonction Exponentielle Terminal

Titre: Résumé : Fonction Exponentielle

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin-bottom:20px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.05em; color:#2c3e50;"><strong>📚 Objectif pédagogique :</strong> Maîtriser la fonction exponentielle : définition, propriétés algébriques, dérivée, variations, limites et applications. Ce résumé couvre les notions exigibles au programme de Terminale.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définition</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Définition - Fonction exponentielle</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Il existe une unique fonction dérivable sur $\displaystyle \mathbb{R}$ notée $\displaystyle \exp$ telle que :</p>
<ul style="margin:5px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li style="margin:5px 0;">$\displaystyle \exp'(x) = \exp(x)$ pour tout $\displaystyle x \in \mathbb{R}$</li>
<li style="margin:5px 0;">$\displaystyle \exp(0) = 1$</li>
</ul>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em style="font-size:0.9em;">Notation : $\displaystyle \exp(x)$ ou $\displaystyle e^x$</em></p>
</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Le nombre $\displaystyle e$</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">$\displaystyle e = \exp(1) \approx 2,718$</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Propriétés algébriques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin-bottom:15px;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème :</strong> Pour tous réels $\displaystyle x$ et $\displaystyle y$ :</p>
</div>
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Produit</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^{x+y} = e^x \times e^y$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Quotient</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^{x-y} = \frac{e^x}{e^y}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Puissance</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle (e^x)^n = e^{nx}$ ($\displaystyle n \in \mathbb{Z}$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Inverse</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^{-x} = \frac{1}{e^x}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Dérivée et variations</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Fonction</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Dérivée</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^x$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle (e^x)' = e^x$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^{ax+b}$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle (e^{ax+b})' = a e^{ax+b}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Variations</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">La fonction exponentielle est <strong>strictement croissante</strong> sur $\displaystyle \mathbb{R}$.</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Pour tous réels $\displaystyle x$ et $\displaystyle y$ : si $\displaystyle x < y$, alors $\displaystyle e^x < e^y$.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Limites et signe</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Valeur</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">Signe</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^x > 0$ pour tout $\displaystyle x \in \mathbb{R}$</td>
</tr>

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Limite en $\displaystyle -\infty$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to -\infty} e^x = 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Limite en $\displaystyle +\infty$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} e^x = +\infty$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Erreurs fréquentes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Piège fréquent</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Correction</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^a \times e^b \neq e^{ab}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">C'est $\displaystyle e^{a+b}$ (additionner les exposants)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle (e^x)^n \neq e^{x^n}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">C'est $\displaystyle e^{nx}$ (multiplier l'exposant)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^a + e^b \neq e^{a+b}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Il n'y a pas de simplification pour la somme</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^x$ peut être négatif</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Non, $\displaystyle e^x > 0$ pour tout $\displaystyle x$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^0 = 0$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Non, $\displaystyle e^0 = 1$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IX. Méthodologie — stratégie de résolution</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(255,165,0,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ffa500 0%, #ff8c00 100%); color:white;">

<tr style="background:#fff8dc;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Simplifier une expression</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Utiliser $\displaystyle e^{a+b} = e^a \times e^b$ et $\displaystyle e^{a-b} = \frac{e^a}{e^b}$</td>

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Résoudre une équation</td>

</tr>

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Résoudre une inéquation</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Utiliser la croissance : $\displaystyle e^a < e^b \Leftrightarrow a < b$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Dériver une fonction</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Pour $\displaystyle e^{ax+b}$ : dérivée = $\displaystyle a e^{ax+b}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fff8dc;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Calculer une limite</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Retenir : $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to -\infty} e^x = 0$ et $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} e^x = +\infty$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
