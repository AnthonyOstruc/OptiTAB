<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Solutions : y' = ay + b</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #dc2626; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(220,38,38,0.15);">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #dc2626 0%, #b91c1c 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:40%;">Équation</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:60%;">Solution générale</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y' = ay + b$ ($\displaystyle a \neq 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y(x) = C e^{a x} - \dfrac{b}{a}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y' = b$ ($\displaystyle a = 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y(x) = bx + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Condition initiale</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y(x_0) = y_0$ détermine $\displaystyle C$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Circuit RC</strong></td>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Second ordre : y'' + ay' + by = 0</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15);">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7e22ce 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:35%;">Discriminant $\displaystyle \Delta = a^2 - 4b$</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:65%;">Solution générale</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \Delta > 0$ : racines $\displaystyle r_1, r_2$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y = C_1 e^{r_1 x} + C_2 e^{r_2 x}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \Delta = 0$ : racine double $\displaystyle r$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y = (C_1 + C_2 x) e^{rx}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \Delta < 0$ : $\displaystyle r = \alpha \pm i\beta$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle y = e^{\alpha x}(C_1 \cos(\beta x) + C_2 \sin(\beta x))$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Erreurs à éviter</h2>

<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px; text-align:center;"><strong>1</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier la constante d'intégration $\displaystyle C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px; text-align:center;"><strong>2</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Erreur de signe dans l'équation homogène : $\displaystyle y' = ay$ donne $\displaystyle Ce^{ax}$ (pas $\displaystyle Ce^{-ax}$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px; text-align:center;"><strong>3</strong></td>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px; text-align:center;"><strong>4</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier d'appliquer la condition initiale pour déterminer $\displaystyle C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px; text-align:center;"><strong>5</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Confondre discriminant 1er ordre ($\displaystyle b^2 - 4ac$) et 2nd ordre ($\displaystyle a^2 - 4b$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px; text-align:center;"><strong>6</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Ne pas vérifier la solution en la substituant dans l'équation originale</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
