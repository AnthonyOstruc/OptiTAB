<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Projeté</strong> de $M$ sur une droite $\Delta$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">Point $H\in\Delta$ tel que $(MH)\perp \Delta$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Distance point-droite</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\text{dist}(M,\Delta)=MH$ (la distance est toujours <strong>perpendiculaire</strong>).</td>
</tr>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Triangle rectangle : Pythagore et trigonométrie</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Outil</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Pythagore</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">Dans un triangle rectangle : $\displaystyle \text{hyp}^2=\text{côté}_1^2+\text{côté}_2^2$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Trigonométrie</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sin=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{hyp}}$ ; $\displaystyle \cos=\frac{\text{adj}}{\text{hyp}}$ ; $\displaystyle \tan=\frac{\text{opp}}{\text{adj}}$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Identité</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \cos^2(\alpha)+\sin^2(\alpha)=1$.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0 0 0; border:2px solid #10b981; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(16,185,129,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Exemple</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Résolution</th>

<tbody>
<tr style="background:#ecfdf5;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">Triangle rectangle en $C$ : $AC=6$ cm et $BC=8$ cm. Calculer $AB$.</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">Par Pythagore : $AB=\sqrt{6^2+8^2}=\sqrt{36+64}=\sqrt{100}=10$ cm.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">Dans ce triangle : calculer $\cos(\widehat{A})$ puis $\widehat{A}$.</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \cos(\widehat{A})=\frac{AC}{AB}=\frac{6}{10}=0{,}6$ donc $\widehat{A}\approx 53^\circ$ (à la calculatrice).</td>
</tr>
</tbody>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Formules utiles (aires, loi des cosinus)</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(16,185,129,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #10b981 0%, #059669 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Situation</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#ecfdf5;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Aire d’un triangle</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \mathcal{A}=\frac{bh}{2}$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Aire avec un angle</strong> (bonus utile)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \mathcal{A}=\frac{ab\sin(C)}{2}$ (côtés $a,b$ et angle compris $C$).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ecfdf5;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Loi des cosinus</strong> (Al-Kashi)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #a7f3d0; padding:14px 16px;">Dans $\triangle ABC$ : $\displaystyle a^2=b^2+c^2-2bc\cos(A)$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Volume prisme / cylindre</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle V=\mathcal{A}_{\text{base}}\times h$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ecfdf5;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Volume pyramide / cône</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle V=\frac{1}{3}\mathcal{A}_{\text{base}}\times h$.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
