===Résumé Dérivation

Titre: Résumé : Dérivation - Formules Usuelles et Règles de Calcul

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définition du nombre dérivé</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Notion</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Nombre dérivé en $\displaystyle a$</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f'(a) = \lim_{h \to 0} \dfrac{f(a+h) - f(a)}{h}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Formule équivalente</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f'(a) = \lim_{x \to a} \dfrac{f(x) - f(a)}{x - a}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Interprétation géométrique</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Coefficient directeur de la tangente à la courbe au point $\displaystyle (a, f(a))$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Équation de la tangente</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle y = f'(a)(x - a) + f(a)$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<strong>Quotient :</strong> Si $\displaystyle f(x) = \dfrac{x}{x+1}$, alors $\displaystyle f'(x) = \dfrac{1 \times (x+1) - x \times 1}{(x+1)^2} = \dfrac{1}{(x+1)^2}$

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Dérivée d'une fonction composée</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Règle générale</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Si $\displaystyle f = g \circ u$, alors $\displaystyle f'(x) = u'(x) \times g'(u(x))$<br><br>
Autrement dit : $\displaystyle [g(u(x))]' = u'(x) \times g'(u(x))$
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">

</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u^n$ ($\displaystyle n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\displaystyle n \geq 1$)</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle n \times u' \times u^{n-1}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \dfrac{1}{u}$ (si $\displaystyle u \neq 0$)</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle -\dfrac{u'}{u^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{u}$ (si $\displaystyle u > 0$)</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \dfrac{u'}{2\sqrt{u}}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle e^{u}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u' \times e^{u}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \ln(u)$ (si $\displaystyle u > 0$)</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \dfrac{u'}{u}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \sin(u)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle u' \times \cos(u)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \cos(u)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle -u' \times \sin(u)$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Exemples pratiques</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
<strong>1.</strong> $\displaystyle f(x) = (3x + 1)^5$ → $\displaystyle f'(x) = 5 \times 3 \times (3x+1)^4 = 15(3x+1)^4$<br><br>
<strong>2.</strong> $\displaystyle f(x) = e^{2x}$ → $\displaystyle f'(x) = 2 \times e^{2x} = 2e^{2x}$<br><br>
<strong>3.</strong> $\displaystyle f(x) = \ln(x^2 + 1)$ → $\displaystyle f'(x) = \dfrac{2x}{x^2 + 1}$<br><br>
<strong>4.</strong> $\displaystyle f(x) = \sin(5x)$ → $\displaystyle f'(x) = 5\cos(5x)$<br><br>
<strong>5.</strong> $\displaystyle f(x) = \sqrt{x^2 + 3}$ → $\displaystyle f'(x) = \dfrac{2x}{2\sqrt{x^2 + 3}} = \dfrac{x}{\sqrt{x^2 + 3}}$
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Applications : sens de variation</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:40%;">Signe de $\displaystyle f'(x)$</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:60%;">Variation de $\displaystyle f$</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f'(x) > 0$ sur un intervalle $\displaystyle I$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f$ est <strong>strictement croissante</strong> sur $\displaystyle I$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f'(x) < 0$ sur un intervalle $\displaystyle I$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f$ est <strong>strictement décroissante</strong> sur $\displaystyle I$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f'(x) = 0$ sur un intervalle $\displaystyle I$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f$ est <strong>constante</strong> sur $\displaystyle I$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f'(a) = 0$ et $\displaystyle f'$ change de signe en $\displaystyle a$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle f$ admet un <strong>extremum local</strong> en $\displaystyle a$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Erreurs fréquentes à éviter</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ef4444; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:50%;">❌ Erreur fréquente</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:50%;">✓ Correction</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (uv)' = u'v'$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (uv)' = u'v + uv'$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (u+v)' = u' \times v'$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (u+v)' = u' + v'$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (e^u)' = e^u$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (e^u)' = u' \times e^u$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (\ln u)' = \dfrac{1}{u}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (\ln u)' = \dfrac{u'}{u}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (\cos x)' = \sin x$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (\cos x)' = -\sin x$ (signe négatif !)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Oublier le carré au dénominateur : $\displaystyle \left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)' = \dfrac{u'v - uv'}{v}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)' = \dfrac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (x^n)' = x^{n-1}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle (x^n)' = nx^{n-1}$ (ne pas oublier le coefficient $\displaystyle n$)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VIII. Méthode de calcul pas à pas</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ffa500; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Type de fonction</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:70%;">Méthode</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Fonction simple</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Appliquer directement la formule du tableau des dérivées usuelles</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Somme/différence</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Dériver terme par terme : $\displaystyle (u \pm v)' = u' \pm v'$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">1. Identifier $\displaystyle u$ et $\displaystyle v$<br>2. Calculer $\displaystyle u'$ et $\displaystyle v'$<br>3. Appliquer $\displaystyle (uv)' = u'v + uv'$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">1. Identifier $\displaystyle u$ (numérateur) et $\displaystyle v$ (dénominateur)<br>2. Calculer $\displaystyle u'$ et $\displaystyle v'$<br>3. Appliquer $\displaystyle \left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)' = \dfrac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Composée</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">1. Identifier la fonction extérieure $\displaystyle g$ et la fonction intérieure $\displaystyle u$<br>2. Calculer $\displaystyle u'$<br>3. Appliquer $\displaystyle [g(u)]' = u' \times g'(u)$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Exemple détaillé : $\displaystyle f(x) = \dfrac{x^2 + 1}{e^x}$</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
<strong>Étape 1 :</strong> Identifier $\displaystyle u = x^2 + 1$ et $\displaystyle v = e^x$<br><br>
<strong>Étape 2 :</strong> Calculer les dérivées :<br>
$\displaystyle u' = 2x$ et $\displaystyle v' = e^x$<br><br>
<strong>Étape 3 :</strong> Appliquer la formule du quotient :<br>
$\displaystyle f'(x) = \dfrac{u'v - uv'}{v^2} = \dfrac{2x \times e^x - (x^2 + 1) \times e^x}{(e^x)^2}$<br><br>
<strong>Étape 4 :</strong> Simplifier :<br>
$\displaystyle f'(x) = \dfrac{e^x(2x - x^2 - 1)}{e^{2x}} = \dfrac{2x - x^2 - 1}{e^x} = \dfrac{-x^2 + 2x - 1}{e^x}$
</td>
</tr>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IX. Formules essentielles à retenir</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e3f2fd; border-radius:8px; padding:20px; margin:15px 0; box-shadow:0 2px 5px rgba(0,0,0,0.1);">
<h3 style="color:#1976d2; margin-top:0;">Les 7 formules d'or</h3>
<ol style="line-height:2; font-size:1.05em;">
<li>$\displaystyle (x^n)' = nx^{n-1}$</li>
<li>$\displaystyle (e^x)' = e^x$ et $\displaystyle (e^u)' = u'e^u$</li>
<li>$\displaystyle (\ln x)' = \dfrac{1}{x}$ et $\displaystyle (\ln u)' = \dfrac{u'}{u}$</li>
<li>$\displaystyle (\sin x)' = \cos x$ et $\displaystyle (\cos x)' = -\sin x$</li>
<li>$\displaystyle (uv)' = u'v + uv'$ (formule du produit)</li>
<li>$\displaystyle \left(\dfrac{u}{v}\right)' = \dfrac{u'v - uv'}{v^2}$ (formule du quotient)</li>
<li>$\displaystyle [g(u)]' = u' \times g'(u)$ (dérivée composée)</li>
</ol>
