===Résumé Primitives

Titre: Résumé : Primitives

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin-bottom:20px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.05em; color:#2c3e50;"><strong>📚 Objectif pédagogique :</strong> Maîtriser les primitives : définition, propriétés, méthodes de calcul (intégration par parties, changement de variable).</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définition</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Définition - Primitive d'une fonction</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Une primitive d'une fonction $\displaystyle f$ définie sur un intervalle $\displaystyle I$ est une fonction $\displaystyle F$ telle que :</p>
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:3px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:400px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle F'(x) = f(x)$ pour tout $\displaystyle x \in I$</p>
</div>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">L'ensemble des primitives se note :</p>
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:3px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:300px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle\int f(x) \, dx = F(x) + C$</p>
</div>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em style="font-size:0.9em;">où $\displaystyle C$ est une constante réelle arbitraire</em></p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Primitives usuelles</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Fonction $\displaystyle f(x)$</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Primitive $\displaystyle F(x) + C$</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle k$ (constante)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle k x + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle x^n$ ($\displaystyle n \neq -1$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \frac{1}{x}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln|x| + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \frac{1}{\sqrt{x}}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle 2\sqrt{x} + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^x$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^x + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sin(x)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle -\cos(x) + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \cos(x)$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sin(x) + C$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Propriétés</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Linéarité de l'intégration</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Pour toutes fonctions $\displaystyle f$ et $\displaystyle g$ admettant des primitives et tous réels $\displaystyle a, b$ :</p>
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border:3px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:500px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle\int (a f(x) + b g(x)) \, dx = a \int f(x) \, dx + b \int g(x) \, dx$</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Intégration par parties</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Intégration par parties</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Si $\displaystyle u$ et $\displaystyle v$ sont des fonctions dérivables, alors :</p>
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border:3px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:500px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle\int u(x) v'(x) \, dx = u(x) v(x) - \int u'(x) v(x) \, dx$</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-left:4px solid #ffa500; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Méthode - Choix de $\displaystyle u$</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Mnémonique <strong>"ILATE"</strong> : Inverse, Logarithme, Algébrique, Trigonométrique, Exponentielle (ordre de priorité pour choisir $\displaystyle u$)</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Changement de variable</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Changement de variable</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">On pose $\displaystyle u = g(x)$ où $\displaystyle g$ est une fonction dérivable, alors $\displaystyle du = g'(x) \, dx$</p>
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border:3px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:400px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle\int f(g(x)) \cdot g'(x) \, dx = \int f(u) \, du$</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Primitives composées</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Fonction composée</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Primitive</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle (ax + b)^n$ ($\displaystyle n \neq -1$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\frac{(ax + b)^{n+1}}{a(n+1)} + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\frac{1}{ax + b}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\frac{1}{a} \ln|ax + b| + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^{ax + b}$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\frac{1}{a} e^{ax + b} + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sin(ax + b)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle -\frac{1}{a} \cos(ax + b) + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \cos(ax + b)$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\frac{1}{a} \sin(ax + b) + C$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0; padding-left:15px; font-size:0.9em; color:#666;"><em>Règle générale : Pour $\displaystyle\int f(ax + b) \, dx$, on divise par le coefficient $\displaystyle a$ et on compose avec $\displaystyle (ax + b)$.</em></p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Erreurs fréquentes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Piège fréquent</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Correction</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Oublier la constante $\displaystyle C$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Toujours ajouter $\displaystyle + C$ à la fin</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Mauvais choix de $\displaystyle u$ dans l'intégration par parties</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Utiliser la mnémonique "ILATE"</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Confondre $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x^2} dx$ et $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x} dx$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x^2} dx = -\frac{1}{x} + C$<br>$\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x} dx = \ln|x| + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Appliquer $\displaystyle\int x^n dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}$ pour $\displaystyle n = -1$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Pour $\displaystyle n = -1$, c'est $\displaystyle\int \frac{1}{x} dx = \ln|x| + C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Oublier de diviser par $\displaystyle a$ dans les primitives composées</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Pour $\displaystyle\int f(ax + b) dx$, diviser par $\displaystyle a$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VIII. Formules essentielles — mémo rapide</h2>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Linéarité</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\int (a f + b g) dx = a \int f dx + b \int g dx$</td>
</tr>

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">Intégration par parties</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\int u v' dx = u v - \int u' v dx$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Changement de variable</td>

</tr>

<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Primitive composée</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle\int f(ax + b) dx = \frac{1}{a} F(ax + b) + C$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
