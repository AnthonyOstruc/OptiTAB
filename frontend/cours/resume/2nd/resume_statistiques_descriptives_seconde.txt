===Résumé Statistiques Descriptives

Titre: Résumé : Information chiffrée et Statistique descriptive — proportions, évolutions, moyenne, médiane, quartiles, dispersion (Seconde)

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin-bottom:20px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.05em; color:#2c3e50;"><strong>📚 Objectif :</strong> Calculer <strong>proportions</strong> et <strong>pourcentages</strong>, maîtriser les <strong>évolutions</strong> (taux, coefficient multiplicateur, successives, réciproque), construire un <strong>tableau statistique</strong> (effectifs, fréquences, cumulés), calculer <strong>moyenne pondérée</strong>, <strong>médiane</strong>, <strong>quartiles</strong>, <strong>écart interquartile</strong> et <strong>écart type</strong>, et comparer deux séries.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Proportions et pourcentages</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Grandeur</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Proportion</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle p = \frac{n}{N}$ (entre 0 et 1)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Pourcentage</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \% = p \times 100 = \frac{n}{N} \times 100$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Effectif</strong> (retrouver)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle n = p \times N = \frac{\%}{100} \times N$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>% de %</strong> (ensembles inclus)</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \frac{p_1 \times p_2}{100}\%$ de l'ensemble global</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Évolutions</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Notion</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Variation absolue</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \Delta V = V_1 - V_0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Taux d'évolution</strong> (variation relative)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle t = \frac{V_1 - V_0}{V_0}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Coefficient multiplicateur</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle C = 1 + t$ ; $\displaystyle V_1 = V_0 \times C$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Évolutions successives</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle C_{\text{global}} = C_1 \times C_2 \times \cdots \times C_n$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Taux global</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle t_{\text{global}} = C_{\text{global}} - 1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Évolution réciproque</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle C_{\text{réc}} = \frac{1}{C}$ ; $\displaystyle t_{\text{réc}} = \frac{1}{C} - 1$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#fff9e6; border-left:4px solid #f59e0b; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>⚠️ Attention :</strong> Hausse de $\displaystyle t\%$ puis baisse de $\displaystyle t\%$ ≠ retour au départ ! On <strong>multiplie</strong> les coefficients, on n'additionne pas les taux.</p>
</div>

</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Effectifs, fréquences, cumulés</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Grandeur</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule / Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Effectif</strong> $\displaystyle n_i$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">Nombre de fois où la valeur $\displaystyle x_i$ apparaît</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Effectif total</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle N = \sum n_i$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Fréquence</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle f_i = \frac{n_i}{N}$ (entre 0 et 1)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Effectif cumulé</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle N_i = n_1 + n_2 + \cdots + n_i$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Fréquence cumulée</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle F_i = \frac{N_i}{N}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Classes</strong> (continu)</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle [a;b[$ signifie $\displaystyle a \le x < b$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#ecfdf5; border-left:4px solid #10b981; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>💡 Vérification :</strong> Le dernier effectif cumulé doit être égal à $\displaystyle N$. La somme des fréquences vaut 1.</p>
</div>

</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Moyenne pondérée</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Cas</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Moyenne pondérée</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \bar{x} = \frac{\sum n_i x_i}{\sum n_i} = \frac{\sum n_i x_i}{N}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Moyenne simple</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \bar{x} = \frac{x_1 + x_2 + \cdots + x_k}{k}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Moyenne de sous-groupes</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle m = \frac{n_1 m_1 + n_2 m_2}{n_1 + n_2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Moyenne en classes</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \bar{x} \approx \frac{\sum n_i c_i}{N}$ avec $\displaystyle c_i = \frac{a_i + b_i}{2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Linéarité</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">Si $\displaystyle y_i = ax_i + b$ alors $\displaystyle \bar{y} = a\bar{x} + b$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Médiane et quartiles</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Indicateur</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Méthode de calcul</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle Q_1$</strong> (1er quartile)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:14px 16px;">Rang $\displaystyle r_1 = \left\lceil\frac{N}{4}\right\rceil$ — sépare les 25% plus petites</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Médiane</strong> $\displaystyle Q_2$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$N$ impair : rang $\displaystyle \frac{N+1}{2}$ ; $N$ pair : moyenne des rangs $\displaystyle \frac{N}{2}$ et $\displaystyle \frac{N}{2}+1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>$\displaystyle Q_3$</strong> (3e quartile)</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">Rang $\displaystyle r_3 = \left\lceil\frac{3N}{4}\right\rceil$ — sépare les 75% plus petites</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#ecfdf5; border-left:4px solid #10b981; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>💡 Avec un tableau :</strong> Compléter les <strong>effectifs cumulés</strong>, puis chercher la première valeur dont le cumulé est ≥ au rang demandé.</p>
</div>

</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Indicateurs de dispersion</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Indicateur</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule / Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Étendue</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle E = x_{\max} - x_{\min}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Écart interquartile</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \text{EIQ} = Q_3 - Q_1$ (dispersion des 50% centraux)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Variance</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle V = \frac{\sum n_i (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{N}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;"><strong>Écart type</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:14px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sigma = \sqrt{V}$ (même unité que les valeurs)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Interprétation :</strong> Petit $\displaystyle \sigma$ ou EIQ → valeurs <strong>concentrées</strong>. Grand $\displaystyle \sigma$ ou EIQ → valeurs <strong>dispersées</strong>. L'EIQ est moins sensible aux valeurs extrêmes que l'étendue.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IX. Mini-formulaire (ultra rapide)</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">

<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Proportion / %</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle p=\frac{n}{N}$ ; $\displaystyle \%=p\times 100$ ; $\displaystyle n=pN$.</td>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">X. Erreurs à éviter</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">❌ Erreur</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">✅ Correction / réflexe</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Confondre effectif, fréquence et pourcentage</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Effectif = nombre ; fréquence ∈ [0;1] ; % = fréquence × 100.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Baisse de 20% → $\displaystyle C = -0,2$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle C = 1 + (-0,2) = 0,8$ (toujours $\displaystyle C > 0$).</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Additionner les taux pour des évolutions successives</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">On <strong>multiplie</strong> les coefficients : $\displaystyle C_1 \times C_2$.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">+25% puis −25% = retour au départ</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle 1,25 \times 0,75 = 0,9375$ : baisse de 6,25% !</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier d'ordonner la série pour médiane/quartiles</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Toujours <strong>trier</strong> dans l'ordre croissant d'abord.</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Histogramme : comparer les hauteurs (classes inégales)</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Si largeurs ≠, comparer les <strong>aires</strong> (densités).</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
