===Résumé Nombres Complexes Terminal

Titre: Résumé : Nombres Complexes

<div style="background:#f9f9f9; padding:20px; border-radius:12px; font-family:Arial, sans-serif; line-height:1.6;">

<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin-bottom:20px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.05em; color:#2c3e50;"><strong>📚 Objectif pédagogique :</strong> Maîtriser les nombres complexes, leurs calculs et leurs applications géométriques. Ce résumé couvre les notions exigibles au programme de Terminale.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Notations et définitions fondamentales</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Nombre complexe :</strong> $\displaystyle z = x + y\,\mathrm{i}$ où $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ et $\displaystyle \mathrm{i}^2 = -1$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em>Notation :</em> $\displaystyle z \in \mathbb{C}$</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#f8f9fa; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Partie réelle :</strong> $\displaystyle \Re(z) = x$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><strong>Partie imaginaire :</strong> $\displaystyle Im(z) = y$</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#fff9e6; border-left:4px solid #f59e0b; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Égalité :</strong> $\displaystyle z_1 = z_2 \Leftrightarrow \begin{cases} \Re(z_1) = \Re(z_2) \\ Im(z_1) = Im(z_2) \end{cases}$</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#ecfdf5; border-left:4px solid #10b981; padding:12px; margin:15px 0;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:0.95em;"><strong>💡 À retenir :</strong> Un complexe est réel si $y = 0$, imaginaire pur si $x = 0$.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Opérations sur les nombres complexes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Opération</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Addition</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z_1 + z_2 = (x_1 + x_2) + (y_1 + y_2)\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><em>Exemple</em></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle (3+2\mathrm{i}) + (1-4\mathrm{i}) = 4 - 2\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Soustraction</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z_1 - z_2 = (x_1 - x_2) + (y_1 - y_2)\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><em>Exemple</em></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle (3+2\mathrm{i}) - (1-4\mathrm{i}) = 2 + 6\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Multiplication</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z_1 \cdot z_2 = (x_1x_2 - y_1y_2) + (x_1y_2 + x_2y_1)\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><em>Méthode</em></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Développer en utilisant $\displaystyle \mathrm{i}^2 = -1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Division</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \dfrac{z_1}{z_2} = \dfrac{z_1 \cdot \overline{z_2}}{|z_2|^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;"><em>Méthode</em></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Multiplier par le conjugué du dénominateur</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Conjugué et module</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Conjugué :</strong> Si $\displaystyle z = x + y\,\mathrm{i}$, alors $\displaystyle \overline{z} = x - y\,\mathrm{i}$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em>Interprétation :</em> Symétrie par rapport à l'axe réel</p>
</div>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">Double conjugué</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \overline{\overline{z}} = z$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Conjugué d'une somme</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \overline{z_1 + z_2} = \overline{z_1} + \overline{z_2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">Conjugué d'un produit</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \overline{z_1 \cdot z_2} = \overline{z_1} \cdot \overline{z_2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Nombre réel</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z$ réel $\displaystyle \Leftrightarrow \overline{z} = z$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Module :</strong> $\displaystyle |z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em>Interprétation :</em> Distance à l'origine dans le plan complexe</p>
</div>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">Relation fondamentale</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z|^2 = z \cdot \overline{z}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Module d'un produit</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z_1 \cdot z_2| = |z_1| \cdot |z_2|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">Inégalité triangulaire</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z_1 + z_2| \leq |z_1| + |z_2|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Module nul</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z| = 0 \Leftrightarrow z = 0$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#fff9e6; border-left:4px solid #f59e0b; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Inverse :</strong> $\displaystyle \dfrac{1}{z} = \dfrac{\overline{z}}{|z|^2} = \dfrac{x - y\,\mathrm{i}}{x^2 + y^2}$ (si $\displaystyle z \neq 0$)</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Argument et calcul par quadrants</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fff9e6; border-left:4px solid #f59e0b; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Définition :</strong> Pour $\displaystyle z \neq 0$, l'argument principal vérifie $\displaystyle \arg(z) \in ]-\pi, \pi]$</p>
</div>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(245,158,11,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #f59e0b 0%, #d97706 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Quadrant</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Argument</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fff9e6;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>I</strong> ($\displaystyle x > 0$, $\displaystyle y > 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = \arctan\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) \in \left]0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right[$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>II</strong> ($\displaystyle x < 0$, $\displaystyle y \geq 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = \arctan\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) + \pi \in \left]\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \pi\right]$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fff9e6;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>III</strong> ($\displaystyle x < 0$, $\displaystyle y < 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fde68a; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = \arctan\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) - \pi \in \left]-\pi, -\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right[$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>IV</strong> ($\displaystyle x > 0$, $\displaystyle y < 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = \arctan\left(\dfrac{y}{x}\right) \in \left]-\dfrac{\pi}{2}, 0\right[$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Cas particulier</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Argument</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">Axe réel positif ($\displaystyle x > 0$, $\displaystyle y = 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Axe imaginaire positif ($\displaystyle x = 0$, $\displaystyle y > 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">Axe réel négatif ($\displaystyle x < 0$, $\displaystyle y = 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = \pi$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Axe imaginaire négatif ($\displaystyle x = 0$, $\displaystyle y < 0$)</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z) = -\dfrac{\pi}{2}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#f3e8ff; border-left:4px solid #9333ea; padding:12px; margin:15px 0;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:0.95em;"><strong>🟣 Méthode :</strong> Toujours vérifier les signes de $x$ et $y$ pour identifier le quadrant avant de calculer l'argument.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Formule de Moivre et puissances</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Formule de Moivre :</strong> Si $\displaystyle z = |z|(\cos\theta + \mathrm{i}\sin\theta)$, alors :</p>
<p style="margin:8px 0 0 0; text-align:center;">$\displaystyle z^n = |z|^n(\cos(n\theta) + \mathrm{i}\sin(n\theta))$</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#f3e8ff; border-left:4px solid #9333ea; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Formule d'Euler :</strong> $\displaystyle e^{\mathrm{i}\theta} = \cos\theta + \mathrm{i}\sin\theta$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Donc $\displaystyle z = |z| \cdot e^{\mathrm{i}\theta}$</p>
</div>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15);">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">Argument d'un produit</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z_1 \cdot z_2) \equiv \arg(z_1) + \arg(z_2) \pmod{2\pi}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Argument d'un quotient</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg\left(\dfrac{z_1}{z_2}\right) \equiv \arg(z_1) - \arg(z_2) \pmod{2\pi}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">Argument d'une puissance</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z^n) \equiv n \cdot \arg(z) \pmod{2\pi}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Argument du conjugué</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(\overline{z}) \equiv -\arg(z) \pmod{2\pi}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Équations complexes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Équation du second degré :</strong> $\displaystyle az^2 + bz + c = 0$</p>
</div>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Étape</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Méthode</th>

</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>1. Discriminant</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \Delta = b^2 - 4ac$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>2. Si $\displaystyle \Delta \geq 0$</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Solutions réelles classiques</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>3. Si $\displaystyle \Delta < 0$</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \sqrt{\Delta} = \mathrm{i}\sqrt{|\Delta|}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Solutions</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{\Delta}}{2a}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#f3e8ff; border-left:4px solid #9333ea; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Racines n-ièmes de l'unité :</strong> $\displaystyle z^n = 1$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><strong>Solutions :</strong> $\displaystyle z_k = e^{\mathrm{i}\dfrac{2k\pi}{n}}$ pour $\displaystyle k = 0, 1, \ldots, n-1$</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#fff9e6; border-left:4px solid #f59e0b; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Racines n-ièmes :</strong> $\displaystyle z^n = a$ ($\displaystyle a \neq 0$)</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><strong>Méthode :</strong> Écrire $\displaystyle a = |a| \cdot e^{\mathrm{i}\theta}$, puis $\displaystyle z_0 = \sqrt[n]{|a|} \cdot e^{\mathrm{i}\dfrac{\theta}{n}}$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><strong>Solutions :</strong> $\displaystyle z_k = z_0 \cdot e^{\mathrm{i}\dfrac{2k\pi}{n}}$ pour $\displaystyle k = 0, 1, \ldots, n-1$</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#ecfdf5; border-left:4px solid #10b981; padding:12px; margin:15px 0;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:0.95em;"><strong>💡 Propriété :</strong> Les solutions complexes d'une équation à coefficients réels sont conjuguées deux à deux.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">X. Erreurs fréquentes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Piège fréquent</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Correction</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier $\displaystyle \mathrm{i}^2 = -1$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Toujours remplacer $\displaystyle \mathrm{i}^2$ par $-1$ lors des développements</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Confondre conjugué et opposé</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Conjugué : $\displaystyle \overline{z} = x - y\,\mathrm{i}$ | Opposé : $\displaystyle -z = -x - y\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Mauvais quadrant pour l'argument</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Toujours vérifier les signes de $x$ et $y$ avant de calculer l'argument</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Écrire $\displaystyle |z_1 + z_2| = |z_1| + |z_2|$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">C'est faux ! Utiliser l'inégalité triangulaire : $\displaystyle |z_1 + z_2| \leq |z_1| + |z_2|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">Oublier le facteur $\displaystyle |z|^n$ dans Moivre</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z^n = |z|^n(\cos(n\theta) + \mathrm{i}\sin(n\theta))$ - Ne pas oublier $\displaystyle |z|^n$ !</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Ne pas rationaliser le dénominateur</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">Toujours multiplier par le conjugué du dénominateur : $\displaystyle \dfrac{z_1}{z_2} = \dfrac{z_1 \cdot \overline{z_2}}{|z_2|^2}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">XI. Formules essentielles — mémo rapide</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">

<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule essentielle</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Forme algébrique</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z = x + y\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Module</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Conjugué</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \overline{z} = x - y\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Relation fondamentale</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z|^2 = z \cdot \overline{z}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Forme trigonométrique</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z = |z|(\cos\theta + \mathrm{i}\sin\theta)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Forme exponentielle</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z = |z| \cdot e^{\mathrm{i}\theta}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Formule de Moivre</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z^n = |z|^n(\cos(n\theta) + \mathrm{i}\sin(n\theta))$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Formule d'Euler</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle e^{\mathrm{i}\theta} = \cos\theta + \mathrm{i}\sin\theta$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Division</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \dfrac{z_1}{z_2} = \dfrac{z_1 \cdot \overline{z_2}}{|z_2|^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Racines n-ièmes de l'unité</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle z_k = e^{\mathrm{i}\dfrac{2k\pi}{n}}$ pour $\displaystyle k = 0, 1, \ldots, n-1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Inégalité triangulaire</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z_1 + z_2| \leq |z_1| + |z_2|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Module d'un produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle |z_1 \cdot z_2| = |z_1| \cdot |z_2|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Argument d'un produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \arg(z_1 \cdot z_2) \equiv \arg(z_1) + \arg(z_2) \pmod{2\pi}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Distance entre deux points</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle AB = |z_B - z_A|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Milieu d'un segment</strong></td>

</tr>
