<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Opérations sur les limites</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(147,51,234,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #9333ea 0%, #7c3aed 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Opération</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:20px 16px;"><strong>Somme</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:20px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} (f(x) + g(x)) = \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) + \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} g(x)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:20px 16px;"><strong>Somme<br>Exception</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:20px 16px;"><em style="color:#e74c3c;">Forme indéterminée :</em> $\displaystyle +\infty - \infty$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:20px 16px;"><strong>Produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:20px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} (f(x) \times g(x)) = \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) \times \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} g(x)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:20px 16px;"><strong>Produit<br>Exception</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:20px 16px;"><em style="color:#e74c3c;">Forme indéterminée :</em> $\displaystyle 0 \times (+\infty)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f3e8ff;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:20px 16px;"><strong>Quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9d5ff; padding:20px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \frac{\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x)}{\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} g(x)}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:20px 16px;"><strong>Quotient<br>Exception</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:20px 16px;"><em style="color:#e74c3c;">Formes indéterminées :</em> $\displaystyle \frac{0}{0}$ ou $\displaystyle \frac{\infty}{\infty}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-left:4px solid #ffa500; padding:18px; margin:20px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<p style="margin:0 0 12px 0; font-size:1.1em;"><strong>⚠️ Formes indéterminées (FI) :</strong></p>
<p style="margin:0 0 15px 0; line-height:1.6;">"Forme indéterminée" signifie qu'on ne peut pas conclure directement, il faut <strong>transformer</strong> l'expression avant d'appliquer les règles.</p>
<div style="display:grid; grid-template-columns:auto 1fr; gap:10px 15px; align-items:center; margin-left:10px;">
<span style="font-weight:bold; color:#f59e0b;">$\displaystyle +\infty - \infty$</span> <span style="font-weight:bold; color:#6c757d;">→ Factoriser par le terme dominant</span>
<span style="font-weight:bold; color:#f59e0b;">$\displaystyle 0 \times \infty$</span> <span style="font-weight:bold; color:#6c757d;">→ Transformer en quotient ou simplifier</span>
<span style="font-weight:bold; color:#f59e0b;">$\displaystyle \frac{0}{0}$</span> <span style="font-weight:bold; color:#6c757d;">→ Factoriser ou simplifier</span>
<span style="font-weight:bold; color:#f59e0b;">$\displaystyle \frac{\infty}{\infty}$</span> <span style="font-weight:bold; color:#6c757d;">→ Factoriser par le terme de plus haut degré</span>
</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Composition des limites :</strong> Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} g(x) = b$ et $\displaystyle \lim_{y \to b} f(y) = L$, alors :</p>
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border:3px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:300px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(g(x)) = L$</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Limites et comparaison</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème des gendarmes :</strong> Soient $f$, $g$ et $h$ trois fonctions telles que, au voisinage de $a$ :</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">$\displaystyle g(x) \leq f(x) \leq h(x)$</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} g(x) = \lim_{x \to a} h(x) = L$ (où $L \in \mathbb{R}$), alors :</p>
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border:3px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:300px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$</p>
</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Comparaison et limite infinie :</strong> Si $f(x) \leq g(x)$ au voisinage de $a$ :</p>
<ul style="margin:5px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li style="margin:5px 0;">Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = +\infty$, alors $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} g(x) = +\infty$</li>
<li style="margin:5px 0;">Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} g(x) = -\infty$, alors $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = -\infty$</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Asymptotes parallèles aux axes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Asymptote horizontale :</strong> Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} f(x) = L$ ou $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to -\infty} f(x) = L$ (où $L \in \mathbb{R}$), alors la droite d'équation <strong>$y = L$</strong> est une asymptote horizontale.</p>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Asymptotes obliques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Définition :</strong> Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} [f(x) - (ax + b)] = 0$ (où $a, b \in \mathbb{R}$ avec $a \neq 0$), alors la droite d'équation <strong>$y = ax + b$</strong> est une asymptote oblique.</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-left:4px solid #ffa500; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Méthode pour déterminer une asymptote oblique :</strong></p>
<ol style="margin:5px 0 0 0; padding-left:20px;">
<li style="margin:5px 0;"><strong>Calculer $a$ :</strong> $\displaystyle a = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{f(x)}{x}$</li>
<li style="margin:5px 0;"><strong>Calculer $b$ :</strong> $\displaystyle b = \lim_{x \to +\infty} [f(x) - ax]$</li>
<li style="margin:5px 0;"><strong>Vérifier :</strong> Si $a \neq 0$ et $b$ existe, alors $y = ax + b$ est asymptote oblique</li>
</ol>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VIII. Méthodes de calcul de limites</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#f8f9fa; border-left:4px solid #6c757d; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Factorisation du terme prépondérant :</strong> Pour calculer une limite à l'infini, factoriser par le terme de plus haut degré au numérateur et au dénominateur.</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em>Exemple :</em> $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{2x^2 + 3x - 1}{3x^2 - x + 2} = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x^2(2 + \frac{3}{x} - \frac{1}{x^2})}{x^2(3 - \frac{1}{x} + \frac{2}{x^2})} = \frac{2}{3}$</p>
</div>

<div style="background:#f8f9fa; border-left:4px solid #6c757d; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Simplification :</strong> Pour les formes indéterminées $\displaystyle \frac{0}{0}$, factoriser numérateur et dénominateur puis simplifier.</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em>Exemple :</em> $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{x^2 - 1}{x - 1} = \lim_{x \to 1} \frac{(x-1)(x+1)}{x-1} = \lim_{x \to 1} (x+1) = 2$</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">X. Formules essentielles — mémo rapide</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Notion</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule essentielle</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Limite d'une somme</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} (f(x) + g(x)) = \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) + \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} g(x)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Limite d'un produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} (f(x) \times g(x)) = \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x) \times \displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} g(x)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Limite d'un quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} \frac{f(x)}{g(x)} = \frac{\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} f(x)}{\displaystyle\lim_{x \to a} g(x)}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Théorème des gendarmes</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Si $g(x) \leq f(x) \leq h(x)$ et $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} g(x) = \lim_{x \to a} h(x) = L$, alors $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Croissance comparée exponentielle</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{e^x}{x^n} = +\infty$ et $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{x^n}{e^x} = 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Croissance comparée logarithme</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\ln(x)}{x^n} = 0$ et $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 0^+} x^n \ln(x) = 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Asymptote horizontale</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:12px 16px;">Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} f(x) = L$, alors $y = L$ est asymptote horizontale</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Asymptote verticale</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:12px 16px;">Si $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a^+} f(x) = \pm\infty$, alors $x = a$ est asymptote verticale</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;"><strong>Asymptote oblique</strong></td>
<td style="border:none; padding:12px 16px;">$\displaystyle a = \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{f(x)}{x}$ et $\displaystyle b = \lim_{x \to +\infty} [f(x) - ax]$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>
