    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définitions essentielles</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Type de limite</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Notation</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Signification</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Limite finie en un point</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">La fonction se rapproche de $\displaystyle L$ quand $\displaystyle x$ tend vers $\displaystyle a$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Limite à l'infini</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to \pm\infty} f(x) = L$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Comportement asymptotique de la fonction</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Limite infinie</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = \pm\infty$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">La fonction devient arbitrairement grande</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
    </div>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Opérations sur les limites</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:25%;">Opération</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Condition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Somme</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim (f + g) = \lim f + \lim g$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Toujours valide</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Produit</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim (f \times g) = \lim f \times \lim g$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Toujours valide</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Quotient</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim \frac{f}{g} = \frac{\lim f}{\lim g}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim g \neq 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Constante</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim (k \cdot f) = k \cdot \lim f$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Toujours valide</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
    </div>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Asymptotes</h2>

    <h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Théorème des gendarmes</h2>
    
    <div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Élément</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Hypothèse</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle g(x) \leq f(x) \leq h(x)$ pour tout $\displaystyle x$ proche de $\displaystyle a$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Condition</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} g(x) = \lim_{x \to a} h(x) = L$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Conclusion</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to a} f(x) = L$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
