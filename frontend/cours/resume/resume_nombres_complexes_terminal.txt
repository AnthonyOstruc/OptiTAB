<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définition et forme algébrique</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Concept</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Nombre complexe</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle z = x + y\,\mathrm{i}$ où $x,y \in \mathbb{R}$ et $\mathrm{i}^2 = -1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Partie réelle</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\Re(z) = x$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Partie imaginaire</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\text{Im}(z) = y$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Ensemble $\mathbb{C}$</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Ensemble des nombres complexes</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Égalité de nombres complexes</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle z_1 = z_2 \Leftrightarrow \begin{cases} \Re(z_1) = \Re(z_2) \\ \text{Im}(z_1) = \text{Im}(z_2) \end{cases}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ffa500; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Cas particuliers</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
<strong>Nombre réel :</strong> $\text{Im}(z) = 0$ → $z = x$<br><br>
<strong>Imaginaire pur :</strong> $\Re(z) = 0$ → $z = y\,\mathrm{i}$<br><br>
<strong>Exemples :</strong> $3 + 4\mathrm{i}$, $-2 + 5\mathrm{i}$, $7$ (réel), $2\mathrm{i}$ (imaginaire pur)
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Multiplication</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle z_1 \cdot z_2 = (x_1x_2 - y_1y_2) + (x_1y_2 + x_2y_1)\,\mathrm{i}$</td>

</tr>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Conjugué et module</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Concept</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Conjugué</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\overline{z} = x - y\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Module</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle|z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#9333ea; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Propriétés du conjugué</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
$\displaystyle\overline{\overline{z}} = z$<br><br>
$\displaystyle\overline{z_1 + z_2} = \overline{z_1} + \overline{z_2}$<br><br>
$\displaystyle\overline{z_1 \cdot z_2} = \overline{z_1} \cdot \overline{z_2}$<br><br>
$\displaystyle z$ est réel $\Leftrightarrow \overline{z} = z$
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #9333ea;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#9333ea; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Propriétés du module</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
$\displaystyle|z|^2 = z \cdot \overline{z} = x^2 + y^2$<br><br>
$\displaystyle|z_1 \cdot z_2| = |z_1| \cdot |z_2|$<br><br>
$\displaystyle|z_1 + z_2| \leq |z_1| + |z_2|$ (inégalité triangulaire)<br><br>
$\displaystyle|z| = 0 \Leftrightarrow z = 0$
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Division et inverse</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Type</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Inverse</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\dfrac{1}{z} = \dfrac{\overline{z}}{|z|^2} = \dfrac{x - y\,\mathrm{i}}{x^2 + y^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Division</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\dfrac{z_1}{z_2} = \dfrac{z_1 \cdot \overline{z_2}}{|z_2|^2}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#f59e0b; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Méthode : multiplier par le conjugué du dénominateur</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
<strong>Principe :</strong> $z \cdot \overline{z} = |z|^2$ (nombre réel)<br><br>
<strong>Exemple :</strong> $\displaystyle\dfrac{2 + \mathrm{i}}{1 - 3\mathrm{i}} = \dfrac{(2 + \mathrm{i})(1 + 3\mathrm{i})}{(1 - 3\mathrm{i})(1 + 3\mathrm{i})} = \dfrac{2 + 7\mathrm{i} - 3}{10} = \dfrac{-1 + 7\mathrm{i}}{10}$
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">La somme des racines n-ièmes de l'unité est nulle : $\displaystyle\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} e^{\mathrm{i}\dfrac{2k\pi}{n}} = 0$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">X. Affixes et applications géométriques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:40%;">Propriété géométrique</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Traduction complexe</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Affixe d'un point $M(x,y)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle z_M = x + y\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Affixe du vecteur $\overrightarrow{AB}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle z_{\overrightarrow{AB}} = z_B - z_A$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Distance entre $A$ et $B$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle AB = |z_B - z_A|$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Milieu $I$ de $[AB]$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle z_I = \dfrac{z_A + z_B}{2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Barycentre $G$ de $(A,\alpha)$ et $(B,\beta)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle z_G = \dfrac{\alpha z_A + \beta z_B}{\alpha + \beta}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Angle orienté $(\overrightarrow{MA},\overrightarrow{MB})$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle\arg\left(\dfrac{z_B - z}{z_A - z}\right) \pmod{2\pi}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:40%;">Ensemble de points</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Description géométrique</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$|z - a| = r$ ($r > 0$)</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Cercle de centre $A(a)$ et rayon $r$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$|z - a| < r$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Disque ouvert de centre $A(a)$ et rayon $r$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$|z - a| = |z - b|$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Médiatrice du segment $[AB]$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\arg(z - a) = \theta$ $[\pi]$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Demi-droite d'origine $A(a)$ et d'angle $\theta$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">XI. Transformations géométriques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">XII. Méthodes pratiques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #f59e0b;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#f59e0b; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:15%;">Étape</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Passage forme algébrique → forme trigonométrique</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>1</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Calculer le module : $\displaystyle r = |z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>2</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Déterminer l'argument : résoudre $\displaystyle\cos\theta = \dfrac{x}{r}$ et $\displaystyle\sin\theta = \dfrac{y}{r}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>3</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Vérifier le quadrant selon les signes de $x$ et $y$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>4</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Conclure : $\displaystyle z = r(\cos\theta + \mathrm{i}\sin\theta)$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #10b981;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Exemple : Convertir $z = -\sqrt{3} + \mathrm{i}$</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
Module : $r = \sqrt{3 + 1} = 2$<br><br>
$\cos\theta = \dfrac{-\sqrt{3}}{2}$, $\sin\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}$<br><br>
Quadrant II → $\theta = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}$<br><br>
Forme polaire : $z = 2\left(\cos\dfrac{5\pi}{6} + \mathrm{i}\sin\dfrac{5\pi}{6}\right)$
</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">XIII. Erreurs fréquentes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ef4444; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Piège à éviter</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Correction</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Oublier que $\mathrm{i}^2 = -1$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Toujours remplacer $\mathrm{i}^2$ par $-1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Confondre conjugué $\overline{z}$ et opposé $-z$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\overline{z} = x - y\,\mathrm{i}$ et $-z = -x - y\,\mathrm{i}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Mauvais quadrant pour l'argument</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Vérifier les signes de $x$ et $y$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Écrire $|z_1 + z_2| = |z_1| + |z_2|$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Faux en général, c'est une inégalité : $\leq$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Oublier le facteur $r^n$ dans Moivre</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$z^n = |z|^n(\cos(n\theta) + \mathrm{i}\sin(n\theta))$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">XIV. Formules rapides — mémo</h2>

<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; border:2px solid #3498db;">
