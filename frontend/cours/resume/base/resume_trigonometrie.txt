<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Cercle trigonométrique et définitions</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db;">

<tr style="background:#3498db; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Notion</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Définition</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Radian</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \pi$ rad $\displaystyle = 180°$ donc $\displaystyle 1$ rad $\displaystyle = \dfrac{180°}{\pi} \approx 57,3°$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Cercle trigonométrique</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Cercle de rayon $\displaystyle 1$ centré à l'origine, orienté dans le sens direct (anti-horaire)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Cosinus</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \cos(x)$ est l'abscisse du point sur le cercle</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Sinus</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \sin(x)$ est l'ordonnée du point sur le cercle</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Tangente</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \tan(x) = \dfrac{\sin(x)}{\cos(x)}$ (définie si $\displaystyle \cos(x) \neq 0$)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Cotangente</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \cot(x) = \dfrac{\cos(x)}{\sin(x)} = \dfrac{1}{\tan(x)}$ (définie si $\displaystyle \sin(x) \neq 0$)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">XVII. Erreurs fréquentes à éviter</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ef4444; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:50%;">❌ Erreur fréquente</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:50%;">✓ Correction</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \cos(a+b) = \cos(a) + \cos(b)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \cos(a+b) = \cos(a)\cos(b) - \sin(a)\sin(b)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \sin(2x) = 2\sin(x)$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \sin(2x) = 2\sin(x)\cos(x)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \sin^2(x) + \cos^2(x) = 0$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \sin^2(x) + \cos^2(x) = 1$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \tan(x) = \dfrac{\cos(x)}{\sin(x)}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \tan(x) = \dfrac{\sin(x)}{\cos(x)}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \arcsin(\sin(x)) = x$ pour tout $\displaystyle x$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \arcsin(\sin(x)) = x$ seulement si $\displaystyle x \in \left[-\dfrac{\pi}{2}, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right]$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Oublier la période dans les solutions d'équations</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Toujours ajouter $\displaystyle + 2k\pi$ ou $\displaystyle + k\pi$ selon le cas</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \cos^{-1}(x) = \dfrac{1}{\cos(x)}$</td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">$\displaystyle \cos^{-1}(x) = \arccos(x)$ ; $\displaystyle \dfrac{1}{\cos(x)} = \sec(x)$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">XVIII. Méthode de résolution d'équations</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:collapse; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:#ffa500; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:30%;">Type d'équation</th>
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px; width:70%;">Méthode</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>$\displaystyle \cos(x) = a$</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">1. Vérifier $\displaystyle |a| \leq 1$<br>2. Utiliser $\displaystyle x = \pm \arccos(a) + 2k\pi$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Avec puissances</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Utiliser les formules de linéarisation pour transformer en équation linéaire</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Type $\displaystyle a\cos(x) + b\sin(x) = c$</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Transformer en $\displaystyle A\cos(x + \varphi) = c$ avec $\displaystyle A = \sqrt{a^2 + b^2}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#ffffff;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;"><strong>Équations polynomiales</strong></td>
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">Poser $\displaystyle t = \cos(x)$ ou $\displaystyle t = \sin(x)$ ou $\displaystyle t = \tan(x)$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<thead>
<tr style="background:#10b981; color:white;">
<th style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:12px;">Exemple : Résoudre $\displaystyle 2\sin^2(x) + \cos(x) - 2 = 0$</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:1px solid #ddd; padding:10px;">
<strong>Étape 1 :</strong> Utiliser $\displaystyle \sin^2(x) = 1 - \cos^2(x)$<br>
$\displaystyle 2(1-\cos^2(x)) + \cos(x) - 2 = 0$<br><br>
<strong>Étape 2 :</strong> Simplifier<br>
$\displaystyle -2\cos^2(x) + \cos(x) = 0$<br><br>
<strong>Étape 3 :</strong> Factoriser<br>
$\displaystyle \cos(x)(-2\cos(x) + 1) = 0$<br><br>
<strong>Étape 4 :</strong> Résoudre<br>
$\displaystyle \cos(x) = 0$ ou $\displaystyle \cos(x) = \dfrac{1}{2}$<br><br>
<strong>Solutions :</strong><br>
$\displaystyle x = \dfrac{\pi}{2} + k\pi$ ou $\displaystyle x = \pm\dfrac{\pi}{3} + 2k\pi$, $\displaystyle k \in \mathbb{Z}$
</td>
</tr>
