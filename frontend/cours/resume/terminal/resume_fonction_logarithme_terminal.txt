<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">I. Définition</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Définition - Fonction logarithme népérien</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">La fonction logarithme népérien, notée $\displaystyle \ln$, est la fonction réciproque de la fonction exponentielle.</p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Pour tout $\displaystyle x > 0$, $\displaystyle \ln(x)$ est l'unique réel tel que :</p>
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border:3px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:300px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle e^{\ln(x)} = x$</p>
</div>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;"><em style="font-size:0.9em;">Pour tout $\displaystyle x \in \mathbb{R}$ : $\displaystyle \ln(e^x) = x$</em></p>
</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">Domaine de définition</td>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Ensemble image</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \mathbb{R}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">II. Propriétés algébriques</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin-bottom:15px;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème :</strong> Pour tous réels $\displaystyle x, y > 0$ et tout rationnel $\displaystyle q$ :</p>
</div>
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Propriété</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Formule</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Produit</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln(xy) = \ln(x) + \ln(y)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Quotient</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln\left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \ln(x) - \ln(y)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Puissance</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln(x^q) = q \ln(x)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Valeurs particulières</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln(1) = 0$ et $\displaystyle \ln(e) = 1$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">III. Dérivée et variations</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Dérivée du logarithme népérien</strong></p>
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border:3px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; padding:15px; margin:12px auto; text-align:center; max-width:250px;">
<p style="margin:0; font-size:1.1em; font-weight:bold;">$\displaystyle (\ln(x))' = \frac{1}{x}$</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Fonction</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Dérivée</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln(x)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \frac{1}{x}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln(u(x))$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \frac{u'(x)}{u(x)}$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#e8f4fd; border-left:4px solid #3498db; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Propriété - Variations</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">La fonction logarithme népérien est <strong>strictement croissante</strong> sur $\displaystyle ]0, +\infty[$.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IV. Limites</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #3498db; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(52,152,219,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #3498db 0%, #2980b9 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Limite</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Valeur</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 0^+} \ln(x)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #d1e7f0; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle -\infty$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} \ln(x)$</td>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#e8f4fd;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 0^+} x \ln(x)$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle 0$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">V. Croissance comparée</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<div style="background:#fee2e2; border-left:4px solid #ef4444; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Théorème - Croissance comparée</strong></p>
<p style="margin:5px 0 0 0;">Pour tout entier naturel $\displaystyle n \geq 1$ :</p>
</div>
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Limite</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Valeur</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">En $\displaystyle +\infty$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} \frac{\ln(x)}{x^n} = 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">En $\displaystyle 0^+$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 0^+} x^n \ln(x) = 0$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p style="margin:10px 0 0 0; padding-left:15px; font-size:0.95em; color:#666;"><em>Le logarithme croît moins vite que toute puissance de $\displaystyle x$</em></p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VI. Équations et inéquations</h2>

<div style="background:#fff8dc; border-left:4px solid #ffa500; padding:12px; margin:10px 0;">
<p style="margin:0;"><strong>Méthode - Résoudre les équations et inéquations logarithmiques</strong></p>
</div>
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(255,165,0,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Équation : $\displaystyle \ln(a) = \ln(b)$</td>

<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Équation : $\displaystyle \ln(x) = k$</td>

</tr>
<tr style="background:#fff8dc;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Inéquation : $\displaystyle \ln(a) < \ln(b)$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle a < b$ (avec $\displaystyle a, b > 0$)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">VII. Erreurs fréquentes</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ef4444; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(239,68,68,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ef4444 0%, #dc2626 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Piège fréquent</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Correction</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln(a) + \ln(b) \neq \ln(a + b)$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">C'est $\displaystyle \ln(ab)$ (produit, pas somme)</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Oublier le numérateur dans la dérivée</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle (\ln(u))' = \frac{u'}{u}$, pas $\displaystyle \frac{1}{u}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fee2e2;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Penser que $\displaystyle \ln(x)$ existe pour $\displaystyle x \leq 0$</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #fecaca; padding:18px 16px;">Le logarithme n'existe que pour $\displaystyle x > 0$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Oublier que $\displaystyle \ln(1) = 0$</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">$\displaystyle \ln(1) = 0$ (et non 1)</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#2c3e50; border-bottom:2px solid #3498db; padding-bottom:8px;">IX. Méthodologie — stratégie de résolution</h2>

<div style="background:#ffffff; border:1px solid #e1e8ed; padding:15px; margin:15px 0; border-radius:6px;">
<table style="width:100%; border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0; margin:15px 0; border:2px solid #ffa500; border-radius:8px; overflow:hidden; box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(255,165,0,0.15); table-layout:fixed;">
<thead>
<tr style="background:linear-gradient(135deg, #ffa500 0%, #ff8c00 100%); color:white;">
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Type de problème</th>
<th style="border:none; padding:14px 16px; text-align:left; font-weight:600; font-size:1.05em; width:50%;">Méthode recommandée</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr style="background:#fff8dc;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Simplifier une expression</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Utiliser $\displaystyle \ln(xy) = \ln(x) + \ln(y)$ et $\displaystyle \ln\left(\frac{x}{y}\right) = \ln(x) - \ln(y)$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Résoudre une équation</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Ramener à $\displaystyle \ln(a) = \ln(b)$ ou $\displaystyle \ln(x) = k$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fff8dc;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Résoudre une inéquation</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #ffe4b5; padding:18px 16px;">Utiliser la croissance : $\displaystyle \ln(a) < \ln(b) \Leftrightarrow a < b$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#f8f9fa;">
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Dériver une fonction</td>
<td style="border:none; border-bottom:1px solid #e9ecef; padding:18px 16px;">Pour $\displaystyle \ln(u(x))$ : dérivée = $\displaystyle \frac{u'(x)}{u(x)}$</td>
</tr>
<tr style="background:#fff8dc;">
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Calculer une limite</td>
<td style="border:none; padding:18px 16px;">Retenir : $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to 0^+} \ln(x) = -\infty$ et $\displaystyle \lim_{x \to +\infty} \ln(x) = +\infty$</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
